Public models selected for your AI systems, infrastructure security and autonomous-vision work. These are source-asset previews, not proposed finished renders.

Recommendation: the concept vehicle is the most direct connection to your EcoCar work. The robotic hand is the more unexpected artistic choice. A server rack best explains your Apple and Splunk systems.

Concept vehicle

EcoCar, ADAS, C++, ROS 2 and YOLO. Open panels, separate wheels, then switch to an illustrative perception mode.
